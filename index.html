00, The cycle

Three jobs. One record per stone.

Compliance proves the stone is not from a sanctioned source and files the statement customs demands. Quoting prices it off live market data, expected yield, and the duty its polishing country sets. Rough cut optimization maps the plan that decides how much of it survives. Ten people build that passport by hand in seven to nine days. This builds it in minutes, off one record, and every field carries the document it came from.

Compliance

Proves origin, files the DDS

Pulls each field from the mining certificate, the lab report and the invoice, checks all three against each other, and blocks on a mismatch. Since 1 January 2026 every natural polished diamond of 0.5 ct or more entering the EU carries this filing.

Wrong origin field: up to 40% of shipment value plus seizure. Sanctioned stone: $377,700 per violation or twice the transaction value, whichever is greater.

Quoting

Prices rough and polished

Live market basis against expected yield. Where a stone was polished now decides its US import tax, so a price cannot be right without the compliance data. Same record, so it always is.

A price that disagrees with the filing is a price that cannot ship.

Rough cut optimization

Maps the plan

Segments the scanned rough, maps every internal feature, and searches the placement space for the highest value recovery under the inclusion constraints.

Only 40 to 42 of every 100 rough carats survive as polished, so the plan quietly decides about 5% of a factory's production.

Run the whole cycle

One click: opens a shared session, brings the cutting floor and compliance into it, plans the cut, takes the cutting report back, files the statement, prices the stone, then blocks one that should never ship.
